## Design lessons

Taken from how each product describes and shows its interface. Live screenshots could not be opened from here; a visual review on your screen is planned for Phase 05.

**Borrow:**

| Pattern | Seen in | Use in Menab |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI proposes, shows a preview, you approve | Filmustage ("You describe the goal. You approve.") | Every regeneration shows before and after; nothing replaces approved work without a click |
| Reusable cast and props that stay consistent | LTX "Elements", Popcorn "Star Cast, Scene Cast, Prop Cast" | The Libraries and Reference lock, with a visible "locked" badge |
| Client review with no sign-up | Boords | The Stage 2 client review link |
| Two-column AV script with automatic voiceover timing | Boords, Storyflow (15, 30, 60 second cuts) | The script output, timed to the chosen length |
| Call sheets that track who opened and confirmed | StudioBinder | Call sheet sending in a later version |
| Dialect choice for voice and lip sync | Popcorn (Gulf, Saudi, Egyptian, Levantine) | Dialect picker on the script and voice outputs |
| Credit balance always visible, updated after each action | LTX | The header balance and cost-before-action rule |
| Phone apps for the set | Studiovity (iOS, Android) | Call sheets readable on a phone |

**Avoid:**

- **The empty canvas.** Storyflow's infinite board suits planners; most clients need the guided stage screen instead.
- **Credit plans that expire monthly.** LTX and Higgsfield sell monthly credits; Menab's pay-per-use is a clear selling point, so show it plainly.
- **Dark, cinematic-only styling.** LTX, Popcorn, Filmustage, and Studiovity all use dark sites. A light, clean, document-like look would stand apart and suit paperwork; offer dark mode as an option.

## What this means for Menab

**Position:** the only Arabic-first platform that takes a project from brief to delivery, whether it is made with AI, shot by a production house, or both. The claim to lead with is the one no competitor can make: "From brief to call sheet, or brief to final shot, in one place."

**Prioritise in v1:**

1. **The brief and strategy stage.** Only Storyflow has one; it is Menab's clearest first impression.
2. **Paperwork in Saudi formats.** CPS, PPM booklet, call sheets, and GAMR permit data. No AI competitor has these.
3. **Saudi and Gulf dialects** in script and voice, to match Popcorn on its strongest point.
4. **Pay-per-use pricing with cost shown before each action,** against the credit plans everyone else sells.

**Watch:** Popcorn AI Studio. It has funding, Arabic dialects, and production-house positioning. If it adds paperwork, it becomes a direct rival. LTX is the strongest AI studio globally and already sells to agencies such as McCann.

**Can wait:** the timeline editor (LTX, Ciaro, and Popcorn already do this well) and call-sheet tracking (StudioBinder).
